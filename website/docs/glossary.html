Glossary

Plain meanings for words you will meet.

WordMeaning
AURThe coin of AUREN.
SparkThe smallest piece of AUR. 1 AUR = 1,000,000,000 sparks.
WalletThe app that holds your keys and lets you send and receive.
AddressLike a bank account number for receiving AUR. Starts with aur1, taur1 or daur1. Safe to share.
Recovery keyThe secret that can rebuild your wallet. Never share it.
NodeA computer that keeps a copy of the network and checks every payment.
ValidatorA node that also confirms new blocks and is paid for it.
BlockA batch of payments confirmed together, about every 1 to 2 seconds.
TransactionA payment or action. The wallet calls them "payments".
FeeA small payment to the network for confirming yours. It is burned.
BurnRemoving coins from existence for good.
StakingLocking AUR behind a validator to earn rewards.
UnbondingThe waiting period before staked coins come back.
CommissionThe part of staking rewards a validator keeps.
FaucetA button that gives you coins on the devnet and testnet.
Devnet / Testnet / MainnetThe three networks. Mainnet is the real one.
ExplorerA website to look up blocks, payments, addresses and names.
Name / domainAn easy name for an address, like alice.aur.
TLD / name endingThe last part of a name: .aur, .david. You can create your own.
Trusted helpersPeople or wallets that can help you recover your account (called guardians in the technical docs).
Daily limitThe most that can leave your account per day.
Proposal / governanceA suggested change to network settings that stakers vote on.
EpochA period of blocks. Node rewards are paid per epoch.
Node rewardAUR paid to nodes that prove they store the chain.
EVMThe system that runs smart contracts, compatible with Ethereum.
Smart contractA program that lives on the network.
RPCThe way apps talk to a node.
SDKA code library for building apps that use AUREN.
